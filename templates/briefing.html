Study briefing

Welcome, and thank you for taking part

Before we start, here is what this study is about and what will happen. It takes about two minutes to read. Please read each page carefully.

What this study is about

Counseling is no longer only offered face to face. People can now also talk with chatbots, virtual agents, and social robots. The effects of these counselor variations are not studied very deeply for mental-health check-ins.

This study compares different kinds of counselors, and some of them are robots. We want to understand how people experience a short check-in conversation with a counselor: how comfortable it feels, what you think of the counselor, and whether you would talk to one like it again.

You will meet one counselor, chosen at random. Everyone hears the same questions. We are interested in your honest impressions. We are not testing you, and there are no right or wrong answers.

{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 2 #}

What will happen

The study has three parts and takes about {{ study.duration_minutes }} minutes in total. The bar at the top of the screen shows where you are.

  1. 1 About you about 5 min

    A questionnaire about your background, your experience with technology and robots, and your views on seeking help with stress or mental health.

  2. 2 The conversation about 5 min

    A short check-in conversation with the chosen counselor variation about how you have been doing, video and audio are included. The next page explains how it works.

  3. 3 Your impressions about 8 min

    A questionnaire about the conversation and the counselor you spoke with.

Afterwards: we explain the study in full (about 2 min).

{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 3 #}

How the conversation works

  • Put on the headphones. Before the conversation starts, you will play a test tone and set a comfortable volume.
  • Listen. The counselor speaks to you through the headphones. The conversation follows a set script.
  • Answer by clicking. When the counselor finishes speaking, answer choices appear on the right side of the screen. Click (or tap) the one that feels closest to true for you. The counselor cannot hear you, so you do not need to speak.
  • The counselor responds to your answer and then asks the next question. There are 5 or 6 questions about how you have been feeling, what has been on your mind, how you cope, and the support you have. There is also a summary question at the end.
  • Take your time. There is no time limit. The conversation cannot be paused or replayed, so please give it your full attention while it is running.
  • Stay on this page. Please do not refresh the page, use the browser's back button, or switch to other windows during the study.
{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 4 #}

What we record

We record

  • Your questionnaire answers
  • The answers you choose in the conversation
  • How long you take to choose each answer
  • Which counselor you met
  • Basic technical details, such as the type of web browser

We do not record

  • Your voice
  • Any picture or video of you
  • Your name. Your answers are de-identified.

Your answers are not seen by anyone except the researchers, and they are not used for treatment. They are analysed only as research data.

{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 5 #}

Looking after yourself

Some questions ask about stress, mood, and the support you have. If a question feels uncomfortable, choose the answer that feels closest; you do not need to explain it.

You can stop at any time, for any reason, without penalty. Simply exit the tab, and the study will stop.

This study is not therapy. If you would like to talk to someone about how you are feeling, the support line at the bottom of every page is available at any time. You can also speak to the researcher at any point.

{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 6 #}

Before you begin

Do you have any questions? Please ask the researcher now, before you start. Once you begin, the researcher can still help with anything technical, or if you want to stop.

When you are ready, click Start Part 1. You will begin with the questionnaire about you.
