Purpose of the study

We are studying how people experience short check-in conversations with different variations of conversational counselors. Your answers will help researchers understand what makes people feel comfortable and supported when talking with differing counselors.

What you will do

  1. Answer a short questionnaire about yourself, your experience with technology, robots, and your attitudes toward counseling.
  2. Take part in a brief, pre-scripted check-in conversation (about 5 minutes). The pre-recorded counselor speaks through your headphones, and you respond by choosing from multiple-choice answers on screen.
  3. Answer a questionnaire about your impressions of the conversation.

Your voice and image are not recorded. We record only the answers you click and how long each answer takes.

This is not therapy

The conversation is a research simulation. It is not a clinical assessment, diagnosis, or treatment, and no one will review your answers to provide care. If you would like mental health support, please contact a qualified professional or the resources listed at the bottom of every page.

Risks and discomforts

Some questions ask you to reflect on stress, mood, and social support, which could cause mild discomfort. You may skip the study, pause, or stop at any time without penalty.

Confidentiality

Your responses are stored on a secure, private database, and are de-identified. De-identified data may be shared with other researchers or in public data repositories.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may decline now or withdraw at any time by closing this window. If you withdraw, your data will not be used.
