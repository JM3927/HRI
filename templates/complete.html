About this study

This study compares how people respond to the same check-in conversation delivered by different kinds of therapists. Each participant was randomly assigned to one version: a human therapist, an expressive social robot, or a mechanical robot. The words spoken were identical in every version; only the therapist’s voice and appearance differed.

The conversation was pre-recorded and followed a fixed script that responded to the answer you chose. No one was listening live, and your answers were not reviewed by a clinician. We measured how much people trusted each therapist, how comfortable they felt sharing, and whether they would use such a therapist in the future.

Because other people may still take part, please do not discuss the details of the study with others.
