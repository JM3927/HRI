Counselor pictures for the study briefing ("The counselors in this study" page).

Put exactly these three files in this folder:

    human.jpg     the human counselor
    furhat.jpg    the Furhat robot
    peerbot.jpg   the Peerbot-style robot

Square images work best (about 600 x 600 px, under 300 KB each); others are
cropped to a square around their centre. Until a file is here, its slot on the
briefing page shows a grey placeholder naming the missing file.

To use another format (e.g. .png), change the three src="/static/images/counselors/..."
lines in templates/briefing.html to match.
